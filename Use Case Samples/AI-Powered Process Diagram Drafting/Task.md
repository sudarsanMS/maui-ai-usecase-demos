# Task

Build a single-screen sample application that demonstrates AI-assisted process diagram drafting in Syncfusion .NET MAUI Diagram. The user enters a natural-language process description, the AI produces a structured workflow draft, and the app renders an editable flowchart for review.

The business purpose is to help business analysts, process owners, and operations teams turn plain-language process descriptions into visual workflows. The user problem is that process documentation is often written in prose and takes time to convert manually into a diagram. The expected outcome is a draft diagram that is easy to inspect, edit, and either accept or discard.

Scope boundaries:
- AI generates only a draft workflow, not a final or authoritative process model.
- The user remains responsible for reviewing, editing, and explicitly accepting the draft.
- The app must not automatically publish, execute, approve, or save workflows.
- The app stays a single-screen showcase focused on one AI workflow from text to editable diagram.
